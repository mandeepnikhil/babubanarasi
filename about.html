About us

Why Babu Banarasi
exists.

Banaras makes some of the finest things in India: silk woven by hand over weeks, food with centuries of recipe behind it, and a city that millions travel to feel. Yet the industry behind it is unorganised, hard to discover and slowly dying.

Weavers stay invisible while powerloom copies are sold as Banarasi. Visitors arrive for the journey of a lifetime and too often meet touts, inflated prices and services no one stands behind.

Babu Banarasi brings it together in one trusted place: handloom sarees straight from the loom, stays and experiences we vouch for, and staples from our own fields. One brand, one standard of quality, and a local you can call. Every rupee spent here travels closer to the people who make it, which is how a heritage industry stays alive: not as charity, but as a business that works.

The founder

Mandeep Nikhil

Founder, Babu Banarasi

I grew up in Banaras, in a business community that lives these very trades: silk, hospitality and the bazaars of the old city. I saw up close how extraordinary the craft is, and how little of its value reaches the people behind it.

I also watched visitors come for the city of a lifetime and leave having seen a rushed, overpriced version of it, while good makers stayed invisible and handloom lost ground to cheaper copies.

Babu Banarasi is my answer: help everyone who comes to Varanasi feel it more deeply, with structure and trust, and put real demand behind local makers so these trades have a future.
