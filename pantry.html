The Pantry

From our soil
to your kitchen.

Banaras has always eaten from the land around it. Our pantry brings that home: staples grown on our own farm, with every seed, input and harvest written down so you know exactly where your food began.

  • Grown on our own land, with no middlemen
  • Every batch traceable from field to pack
  • Packed in small batches, shipped across India
