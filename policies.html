Shipping

  • Within India: every saree is sent by insured, tracked courier. Shipping within India is free.
  • When it ships: usually within 3–7 working days after we confirm your order and payment. You'll get the tracking number on WhatsApp.
  • Outside India: we ship worldwide. Because rates and customs vary, we quote shipping on WhatsApp before you pay. Any import duties or taxes in your country are payable by you.
  • Before it leaves: we show you the actual piece on a video call, in daylight, so you know exactly what's coming.

Returns & exchanges

  • If your saree arrives damaged, or isn't the piece you approved on video, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with photos and the unboxing video if you have one. We'll arrange a replacement, repair or refund.
  • Because every handloom piece is shown to you before it ships, we don't take returns for a change of mind.
  • Small irregularities in the weave are a mark of a handloom, not a defect.
  • Made-to-order and customised pieces can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or differ from what we agreed.
  • Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 7 working days of us receiving the piece back.

Payments

  • Order on WhatsApp: you pay nothing when you place the order. Once we confirm the piece on video, we send you a secure UPI or payment link.
  • Pay online: when available, online payments are processed by Razorpay (UPI, cards and netbanking). We never see or store your card details.
  • All prices are in Indian rupees (₹).
  • Please only pay through the links we send from our WhatsApp number, +91 92609 84531.

Made to order

  • For pieces woven to order, we agree the design, colours, price and timeline with you on WhatsApp before the weaver begins.
  • We may ask for an advance to start the weaving, and share photos from the loom as it progresses.
  • Handloom timelines can shift by a few days. If a date matters, tell us at the start and we'll be honest about what's possible.

Privacy

  • What we collect: only what you give us to deliver your order or answer you: name, phone, address and email if you add one, sent to us in your WhatsApp message.
  • How we use it: to confirm, ship and support your order. We don't sell or share your details, except with the courier delivering your parcel and, for online payments, Razorpay.
  • On your device: your bag and checkout details are saved in your own browser so you don't have to type them again. You can clear them by clearing your browser's site data.
  • No tracking: this website uses no advertising or analytics cookies. It shows live Varanasi weather from Open-Meteo and loads fonts from Google Fonts; neither receives your personal details from us.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, message us on WhatsApp.

Terms

  • An order is confirmed only when we've confirmed availability with you and received payment.
  • Each handloom piece is unique. Colours can look slightly different on screens, which is why we show every piece on video first.
  • Products marked "Sample" are illustrations of our range, not pieces for sale. If you're interested, message us and we'll show you what's on the loom.
  • If something goes wrong, we'll always try to put it right first. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
