What to Eat in Varanasi: 10 Banarasi Foods and When to Eat Them

Banaras eats by the hour and by the season. Some of its best dishes exist only on winter mornings or summer afternoons, so here's what to eat, and when to find it.

Breakfast: kachori-sabzi and jalebi

The Banarasi breakfast is eaten standing up in the lanes: puffed lentil-stuffed kachoris with a spicy potato sabzi, followed by jalebi straight from the kadhai and a kulhad of chai.

Malaiyo (winter only)

Malaiyo is milk left out overnight under the winter dew, then whisked into a cloud of foam flavoured with saffron, cardamom and pistachio. It melts on the tongue and disappears by mid-morning. Look for it from roughly November to February.

Choora matar (winter)

Flattened rice tossed with fresh green peas, ghee, spices and a touch of sugar. It's a winter breakfast, best when the peas are young and sweet.

Tamatar chaat (evenings)

Banaras's own chaat: tomatoes cooked down with potato, ginger and spices, served in a leaf bowl and topped with crushed namak-para, sweet syrup and a sprinkle of masala. Sweet, sour, spicy and smoky at once.

Launglata

A crisp pastry filled with khoya, folded shut and pinned with a single clove (laung), then soaked in sugar syrup.

Rabri

Milk simmered for hours until it thickens into soft layers of cream, served cool in a clay kulhad.

Thandai (Holi and summer)

Chilled milk ground with almonds, fennel, rose petals, poppy seeds and black pepper. It flows freely during Holi and Mahashivratri.

Kulhad lassi (warm months)

Thick curd churned by hand, topped with a layer of malai and served in a clay cup that goes back to the earth after you've finished.

Langda mango (June–July)

Banaras's own mango variety, still green-skinned when ripe, sweet and almost fibreless. Banarasi Langda carries a GI tag.

Banarasi paan

A betel leaf folded around gulkand, fennel, katha and other fillings, and the traditional end to a Banarasi meal. Banarasi paan also carries a GI tag.

Questions people ask

What is the most famous food in Varanasi?

Kachori-sabzi with jalebi for breakfast, tamatar chaat in the evening, malaiyo in winter and Banarasi paan after a meal are the city's best-known foods.

When can you eat malaiyo in Varanasi?

Malaiyo is a winter speciality, usually found from about November to February, early in the morning. It is gone by mid-morning.

Is Varanasi food vegetarian?

Most traditional Banarasi street food and sweets are vegetarian, and many places near the temples serve sattvic food without onion and garlic.
